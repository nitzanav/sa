- Recency books: yahoo_and_tipranks_1d_recency through yahoo_and_tipranks_5d_recency. Analyst >0/>1/>2/>3/>4 × projected >0/10/20/30%. Each cell is one book.

- A signal buys the next session's open. Analyst points arrive through the signal day, so that day's close is not a trade. A Friday signal buys Monday's open. The lot sold to make room sells at that same open. Stops still use the close, and they run after the open, so today's close cannot free a slot for this morning's buy. A lot bought at the open can be stopped at that day's close.
- No SPY and no TQQQ. An empty slot stays cash.
- Four strength formulas, each at least 1 lot and at most 16. ls sells the biggest loss. If every stock lot is in profit, the least profitable one is sold only when its strength is strictly below the new signal.
- Loss dropdown: 1.25%, 2.5%, 5%, and 7.5%. Profit protection: 2.5%, 5%, and 7.5% of the gain from entry to the highest close. 10% loss dropdown and 10% profit protection are not in this grid.
- 100 buys × 48 sells = 4,800 cells. Trusted cells have at least 30 signals.

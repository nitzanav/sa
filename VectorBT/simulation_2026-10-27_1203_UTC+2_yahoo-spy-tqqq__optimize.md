## Notes

- yahoo-daily only. Winner buys: analyst >2/>3/>4 × projected >20/30/40/50%.
- Loss dropdown, while the close is below the entry: 1.25dd, 2.5dd, 7.5dd, 10dd from that entry. Profit protection, once a close has traded above the entry: 2.5pp, 5pp, 10pp, 20pp, 30pp of the gain from entry to the highest close. Stops are checked before new signals and do not apply to SPY or TQQQ lots. A new high does not sell.
- Book size is 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, or 20 lots of $5,000. A signal buys at that close when a slot is free. Two signals on the same ticker can both hold a slot.
- A slot still empty after the day's stock buys holds TQQQ. A slot freed by a stop and not reused for a new ticker holds SPY. A new buy sells a SPY lot before it sells TQQQ, and TQQQ before it sells a stock.
- When the book is full of stocks and any lot is at a loss, sell the biggest loss and buy the new signal. When every stock lot is in profit, sell the least profitable one only if its strength is strictly below the new signal. Strength is sqrt(analysts, capped at 10) times the target percent floored to 10-point steps.
- Same-day signals are taken strongest first. A filler that is never sold stays open through the last quote day.
- Trusted cells have at least 30 signals. Smaller cells stay in the matrices.
- Signals dated after --end are dropped.
- Days exposed counts calendar days with at least one lot open. Lot-days sums each lot's calendar hold, so overlapping lots count again. SPY and TQQQ lots are included in the stock P&L.
- Comparison is on invested dollars, not book vs SPY.

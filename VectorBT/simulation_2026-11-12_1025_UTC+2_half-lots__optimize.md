## Notes

- One book of $320,000 split into 256 lots of $1,250. The rank is a×t: analysts × the projected fraction.
- Stops run at the close. The next open counts the lots those stops left free. The strongest signal takes half of them, rounded up. The next signal takes half of what remains, rounded up, and a third signal does the same. With 256 free lots and three signals that is 128, 64, and 32. The last 32 stay cash. One signal takes 128 and leaves 128 cash. Five free lots round up to 3, then 1, then 1.
- A later session can buy a ticker that already has open lots. A full book does not sell a lot to make room. A stop on today's close frees lots for the next open, not for this morning.
- There is no loss dropdown. Profit protection is 5%, 10%, or 15% of the gain from entry to the highest close. A position that never trades above its entry stays open through the last quote.
- The book is yahoo_and_tipranks_any_recency, bought at the next open. Analyst cuts are >0, >2, >3, and >4. Projected cuts are >10%, >20%, >30%, >40%, >50%, and >60%. The window is January–October. A ticker and day on more than one recency book keeps the a×t row. No SPY and no TQQQ.
- 4 analyst cuts × 6 upside cuts × 3 profit protects = 72 cells. Trusted cells have at least 30 signals.

## Notes

- Same daily books and the same winner buys: analyst >2/>3/>4 × projected >20/30/40/50%.
- The book starts with 8 lots of $5,000. A signal buys at that close when a slot is free. Two signals on the same ticker can both hold a slot.
- When the book is full and any lot is at a loss, sell the biggest loss at that close and buy the new signal. Strength is not consulted.
- When every open lot is in profit, sell the least profitable one only if its strength is strictly below the new signal. Strength is sqrt(analysts, capped at 10) times the target percent floored to 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, …. Two analysts at a 33% target score sqrt(2)×30. A flat lot is not a loss and not a profit, so it is not sold.
- Same-day signals are taken strongest first. A lot opened earlier the same day is flat, so a later signal that day cannot replace it.
- No hold, no profit target, and no drawdown. A lot that is never replaced stays open through the last quote day.
- Signal books: yahoo-daily, tipranks-daily, google-daily, all_sources-daily, yahoo_and_google-daily. Each cell is one book.
- Trusted cells have at least 30 signals. Smaller cells stay in the matrices.
- Signals dated after --end are dropped. An open lot runs through the last quote day.
- Days exposed counts calendar days with at least one lot open. Lot-days sums each lot's calendar hold, so overlapping lots count again. Avg lot is the mean dollars used to open a lot.
- Comparison is on invested dollars, not book vs SPY.

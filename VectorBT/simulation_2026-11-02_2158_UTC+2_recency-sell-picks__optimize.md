Each older session weighs 2.5 divided by how many trading days back it is, counting the signal session as 1. The analyst count is that weight sum. The projected upside is the weighted mean. Analyst >0/>1/>2/>3/>4 × projected >0/10/20/30%. Each cell is one book.
- One book of $320,000. Lot count splits that cash: 8 lots are $40,000 each, 64 lots are $5,000 each. Counts are 8/12/16/32/64.
- No SPY and no TQQQ. An empty slot stays cash. A stop that is not reused stays cash. Uninvested money is cash, not a SPY sleeve.
- A ticker that already has an open position is skipped. Lots opened by one signal stay one position. After that position closes, a later signal can open a new one.
- Four strength formulas, each at least 1 lot and at most 16. at is floor(analysts × target percent / 10). sqra is floor(analysts²). a is floor(analysts × 3). sqra_target is floor(analysts² × target percent / 40).
- Sell pick is ls, dls, or nb. ls sells the biggest loss. dls sells that loss divided by calendar days held. If every stock lot is in profit, the least profitable one is sold only when its strength is strictly below the new signal. nb does not sell a lot to free a slot. The signal is bought only when a slot is already free.
- Loss dropdown, while the close is below the entry: 1.25%, 2.5%, 5%, 7.5%, and 10%. Profit protection, once a close has traded above the entry: 2.5%, 5%, 7.5%, and 10% of the gain from entry to the highest close. Those stops still run under nb.
- Win rate is positions, not lots. Lots opened together — the same ticker, buy date, and buy price — are one position, and that position wins when their P&L sums to more than zero. The top 30 table counts Symbols once per ticker and Positions once per entry, even when a symbol has several lots or several signals.
- Lot-days invested is invested lot-days divided by the lot count times the calendar days in the test. A full book held every day is 100%.
- 100 buys × 1,200 sells = 120,000 cells. Trusted cells have at least 30 signals.

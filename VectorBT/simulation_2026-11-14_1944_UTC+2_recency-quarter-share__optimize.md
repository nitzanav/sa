- A fallback step buys a share of the lots that are already free. It does not sell a stock to make room. The share is rounded to the nearest lot. Half a lot rounds up. The day's names share that budget, strongest first. Each name still asks for floor(analysts × target percent / 10) lots and stops when the budget is filled. Every lot is $26,667, so a share of the free lots is the same share of the free money.
- Buy windows are Jan–May, Jun–Oct, and Jan–Oct. Signals are dated inside the window. Prices continue through the last quote, so a position can sell after the buy window.
- 1 step-1 buy + 3 fallback depths × 3 ranges = 12 cells. Trusted cells have at least 30 signals.

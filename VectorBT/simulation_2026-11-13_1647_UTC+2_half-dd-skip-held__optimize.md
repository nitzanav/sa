## Notes

- One book of $320,000 split into 256 lots of $1,250. The rank is a×t: analysts × the projected fraction.
- Stops run at the close. The next open counts the lots those stops left free. The strongest signal that is not already held takes half of them, rounded up. The next new signal takes half of what remains, rounded up, and a third new signal does the same. With 256 free lots and three new signals that is 128, 64, and 32. The last 32 stay cash. One new signal takes 128 and leaves 128 cash. Five free lots round up to 3, then 1, then 1.
- A ticker that already has open lots is skipped. After that position closes, a later signal can buy it again. A full book does not sell a lot to make room. A stop on today's close frees lots for the next open, not for this morning.
- Loss dropdown is 1.25%, 2.5%, 5%, or 10% from the entry while the close is under entry. Profit protection is 5%, 10%, or 15% of the gain from entry to the highest close. A position that never trades above its entry can still hit loss dropdown.
- The book is yahoo_and_tipranks_any_recency, bought at the next open. Analyst cuts are >0, >2, >3, and >4. Projected cuts are >10%, >20%, >30%, >40%, >50%, and >60%. The window is January–October. A ticker and day on more than one recency book keeps the a×t row. No SPY and no TQQQ.
